## Summary

Formulated a safe *Flow Matching* framework inspired by *Control Barrier Functions (CBFs)*, strictly enforcing safety constraints at the terminal trajectory rather than at intermediate generative latents.

The proposed method is currently being validated and benchmarked experimentally against baseline algorithms from the literature.

*This project is ongoing; results will be added on completion.*
