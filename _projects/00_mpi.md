## Project 1: Modeling Asymmetric Vibrations

Designed a lumped-parameter model and performed system identification to simulate asymmetric vibrations of the actuator and developed feedforward controllers to track a reference acceleration profile. Designed and conducted a user study to investigate the effect of grip force on perceived lateral forces when asymmetric vibrations are applied to the actuator.

## Project 2: System Identification of a Novel Fingertip Haptic Actuator

Developed a dynamical model and performed system identification to simulate high-frequency fingertip vibrations induced by the actuator by fitting experimental data to the model.

## Takeaways

Working in the Haptic Intelligence Department was my first experience performing system identification from the ground up — from designing and fabricating the mounts that hold the actuator in place, to choosing the optimal excitation signal, to running an optimizer to fit the data. It was also my first time designing a user study. I additionally took part in a workshop on creating effective scientific figures for academic publications.
