## Aim

Design a Model Predictive Controller to regulate the liquid levels in the four-tank system.

## Summary

Implemented Model Predictive Control in *MATLAB*. Used a Kalman filter and a particle filter for state estimation and compared their performance. Simulated scenarios with different measured variables, control variables, and constraints on the inputs and outputs.

## Takeaways

This project reinforced my understanding of Kalman filtering and model predictive control, and of how the choice of state estimator affects closed-loop performance.
