## Aim

Review the derivation of the Poisson's ratio of auxetic metamaterials.

## Summary

Analysed auxetic metamaterials with a re-entrant honeycomb structure in 2D and 3D. Also analysed synclastic bending and the possible applications of this property.

## Takeaways

I chose this topic because of my interest in materials with unconventional mechanical behaviour. What I valued most was that the analytical expression could be derived almost entirely from concepts covered in the course.
