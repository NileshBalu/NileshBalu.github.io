About Me

I am a Master’s student in the Mechanical Engineering Department at Eidgenössische Technische Hochschule (ETH) Zürich, interested in the dynamics and control of learning-based systems. As learned models increasingly drive how robots plan and act, I am motivated by two questions: how to enrich these systems with additional sources of input and feedback, such as haptics, and how to provide formal safety guarantees for their behavior. These guarantees become critical in safety-critical applications such as autonomous driving and medical robotics.

I am currently working on my Master’s thesis with Prof. Lars Lindemann at ETH Zürich, developing a safe Flow Matching framework for robot trajectory generation inspired by Control Barrier Functions. Before this, I completed a research internship at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, where I focused on the dynamical modeling and system identification of haptic actuators. I am also a Student Assistant for the RobotX Robotics Summer School at ETH Zürich, where I am supporting the transition of the program's primary hardware platform from wheeled robots to quadrupeds.

I completed my Bachelor’s degree at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras, where I graduated at the top of my class and won the Best Thesis Award. During my time there, I developed a speed-control submodule, an autonomous system using ROS, and a prototype of a compliant hand exoskeleton.
