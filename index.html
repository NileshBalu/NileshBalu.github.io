About Me

I am a Master’s student in the Mechanical Engineering Department at Eidgenössische Technische Hochschule (ETH) Zürich, passionate about robotics. I am driven by challenging research questions in this field and the opportunity to make a positive impact on people’s lives.

I am currently working on my Master’s thesis with Prof. Lars Lindemann at ETH Zürich, developing a safe Flow Matching framework for robot trajectory generation inspired by Control Barrier Functions. Before this, I completed a research internship at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, where I focused on the dynamical modeling and characterization of haptic actuators. Currently, I am a Student Assistant for the RobotX Robotics Summer School at ETH Zürich. In this role, I am supporting the transition of the program's primary hardware platform from wheeled robots to quadrupeds.

I completed my Bachelor’s degree at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras, where I graduated at the top of my class and won the Best Thesis Award. During my time there, I developed a speed-control submodule, an autonomous system using ROS, and a prototype of a compliant hand exoskeleton.
