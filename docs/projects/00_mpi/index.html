Dynamical Modeling and System Identification of Haptic Actuators

Project 1. Modeling Asymmetric Vibrations:

Designed a lumped-parameter model and performed system identification to simulate asymmetric vibrations of the actuator and developed feedforward controllers to track a reference acceleration profile. Designed and conducted a user study to investigate the effect of grip force on perceived lateral forces when asymmetric vibrations are applied to the actuator.

Project 2. System Identification of a Novel Fingertip Haptic Actuator:

Developed a dynamical model and performed system identification to simulate high-frequency fingertip vibrations induced by the actuator by fitting experimental data to the model.

Takeaways:

I had an amazing experience working at the Haptic Intelligence Department. This was my first experience performing system identification from the ground up - from designing and fabricating the required mounts to hold the actuator in place, to deciding the optimal excitation signal to use, and finally running an optimizer to fit the data. This was also the first time I was involved in designing a user study. Another key takeaway from my time here was I participated in a workshop on how to create good scientific figures for academic reporting and publications.

Publication in Progress:

Portions of this research are currently being prepared for paper submission to the EuroHaptics 2026 conference.
